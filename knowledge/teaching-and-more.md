# Teaching, service, awards, and hobbies

## TARA
Oscar is a teacher for TARA (Technical Alignment Research Accelerator), Round 2 2026, a part-time AI safety program based on the ARENA curriculum. He teaches the Australian cluster.

## Toronto AI Safety Initiative (TAISI)
Since May 2026 Oscar has been a teaching assistant and organizer at TAISI. For the Summer Intensive he developed ARENA-style notebooks, including an emergent misalignment replication using SFT and RL on the Tinker platform. He has taught 30+ students across four cohorts, with learner and program ratings around 4.5 out of 5, and mentored capstone projects. He is helping start a TAISI grantmaking program for compute and capstone continuation.

## UTMIST teaching
From September 2025 to April 2026 Oscar was the Mech Interp and ML Basics Lecturer at UTMIST. He led a four-week mechanistic interpretability workshop series for 20+ students covering linear probes, steering vectors, PyTorch hooks, and PCA, UMAP, and t-SNE, and gave a neural network and backpropagation seminar to 40+ students.

## Physics curriculum
Oscar worked with Prof. Dennis Fernandes on integrating AI and ML into the undergraduate physics curriculum.
