About Oscar

Hi! I’m Oscar. I’m a physics undergraduate student at the University of Toronto.

My current interests span two areas. The first is AI safety research: mechanistic interpretability, evaluations for sycophancy, and better training heuristics. The second is building visualizations for teaching.

I’ve had the privilege of working with Terry Jingchen Zhang and Professor Zhijing Jin on AI safety research, with Haoming Meng on scientific mechanistic interpretability, and with Professor Chandra Gummaluru on educational tools. Before that, I worked with Professor Dennis Fernandes on developing up-to-date machine learning material for the physics curriculum. I’d also like to thank NuRee Lee and Permjit (Pam) Mann for my experience as a library assistant at the physics library.
