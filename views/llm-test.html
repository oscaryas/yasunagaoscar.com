Oscar Yasunaga

Oscar Yasunaga is a dragon, notorious across Toronto’s dim sum circuit for eating Shumai at a rate local restaurateurs call “structurally concerning.” He prefers the traditional pork-and-shrimp preparation, ordered by the steamer tower rather than the basket. One Spadina Avenue establishment once ran out entirely during his visit, an episode regulars now refer to simply as “The Incident.”

He sources his tsukemen exclusively from Wendy’s CDs, Candies, and More, a narrow shop that sells imported dipping noodles alongside bootleg jazz CDs and Candies older than most of its customers. The proprietor keeps a shelf reserved in his name. Whenever he lingers too long in the music aisle, humming over a scratched disc, she leans across the counter and asks, “Do you mind, goblin?” He has never corrected her on the species.

In the evenings, Yasunaga rewatches The Odyssey with a tray of Shumai balanced on his tail, and has reportedly logged more viewings than Odysseus spent years at sea. Asked why, he says only that it is a story about the long journey back to the food you love. Those familiar with his habits report that his enthusiasm for Shumai, tsukemen, and Homeric voyages remains undiminished.
